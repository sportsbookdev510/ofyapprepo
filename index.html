How it works

Deposit once, then live off the yield.

1

Deposit

Send stablecoins in. They become your locked principal and are supplied to a lending money market on Robinhood Chain.

2

Accrue

Two numbers, nothing else: locked principal, and spendable yield. The spendable number grows as lending interest accrues.

3

Spend

The card draws from spendable yield only. If a charge is larger than your accrued interest, it is rejected outright, never partially filled from principal.

4

Withdraw

Pull your principal back to your wallet whenever you want. It is always a deliberate action you take, never something a spend can trigger.

The one rule

Your principal is never spendable.

Two separate balances

Principal and spendable yield are distinct accounting values. Spendable equals yield accrued minus yield already spent. Nothing else ever feeds it.

Fails closed

If a chain read fails or accrual data is missing, spendable is treated as zero and the spend is rejected. A timeout never unlocks principal.

One lending market

Yield is plain lending interest from a single money market on Robinhood Chain. No strategies, no leverage. Your deposit and its interest never touch the $OFY token.

No partial fills

A charge above your spendable yield is declined whole. There is no fallback path that tops up a shortfall from your deposit.

Withdraw on your terms

Principal leaves only when you explicitly withdraw it, in full or in part, straight back to your wallet.

Proven before value

Every flow (deposit, accrual, spend, withdraw) is proven end to end, then with the smallest possible amounts, before it scales.

Yield lives on Robinhood Chain.

Offyield runs on the USDG-native L2, an Arbitrum Orbit rollup, chain id 4663, EVM-compatible. There is no validator staking here: your yield is lending interest from an on-chain money market, and nothing else.
